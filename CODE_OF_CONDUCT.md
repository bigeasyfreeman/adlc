# Code of Conduct

ADLC is committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community.

Participants are expected to discuss ideas and evidence rather than attack
people; respect differing experience levels and backgrounds; accept correction
gracefully; protect private and security-sensitive information; and avoid
harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or sustained
disruption.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these
standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious or
sensitive concerns should be reported privately through the repository's
security-advisory channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially,
with action proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

These standards apply in repository discussions, reviews, issues, community
spaces, and public representation of the project.
