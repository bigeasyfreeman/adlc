# Governance

ADLC is currently maintainer-led. The repository maintainer owns product-contract changes, support-label promotion, security-release coordination, and final release approval. Contributors may propose changes through issues and pull requests; evidence and compatibility impact are reviewed in public except for coordinated security work.

Public-surface, schema, release, security, and provider-support changes require maintainer review. No contributor role grants authority to merge, publish, deploy, disclose a vulnerability, or spend project funds without an explicit recorded decision.

Material decisions should be recorded in repository documentation or pull-request history. Support claims follow the evidence rules in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md). Community conduct follows [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
